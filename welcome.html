Welcome to Trellis

Keep sensitive data out of AI chatbots.

What Trellis does

  • On AI chat sites (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others you choose), it checks the text you paste, drop or type and replaces personal or confidential data with placeholders such as [EMAIL_1] before the site receives it.
  • When the reply comes back, you can see the original values on hover and copy them, only on your screen.
  • If a message with data you typed is about to be sent without being obfuscated, Trellis stops it.

What Trellis reads, and what it does not do

  • It reads the text you paste, drop or type in the message box, and the replies shown on the chat sites in your list, in order to find sensitive data and show original values.
  • Everything happens in your browser. Trellis has no server, makes no network requests of its own, collects no analytics and shares nothing with anyone, including its developers.
  • Your memory (your own keywords) and your settings are stored only in this browser. The link between placeholders and original values is kept in memory and deleted when the browser closes.
  • It only runs on the chat sites in your list. You can add or remove sites in the settings.

Get started

  1. Reload any chat tab that was already open.
  2. Add the names, companies, clients or projects you want to hide to your memory: in the settings, from the toolbar popup, or by selecting text on any page and choosing Add to Trellis memory.
  3. Paste instead of typing when you can: pasted text is checked before the site ever sees it.
